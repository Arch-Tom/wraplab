ADAPTED: Keep the existing validated stroke-to-filled-outline normalizer. It outlines before warping;
nonlinear mapping and constant-width output stroking do not commute. Filled export paths remain
M/L/Z. Default digital target 0.01 mm is stricter than the suggested 0.02 mm. Packet B's reference
anchor is translated to the common top-center anchor; metadata makes this explicit.

SUPERSEDED: the provisional endpoint-fitted fan was replaced by Packet B's support-wide conformal
family. No curved shape is described as exactly flattenable. Material extension/compression is
1/lambda-1, separate from deliberate front-to-pattern magnification.

DEFERRED: certified cubic refitting, general strain optimization, perspective viewing, material
simulation, automatic cuts/darts/seam clipping of customer paths, arbitrary meshes and machine
control. Intrinsic artwork must stay within its selected unwrapped single-wrap domain; seam-crossing
paths are rejected rather than silently wrapped modulo 2*pi. No automatic redesign/boolean repair.

UNVERIFIED EXTERNAL GATES: native Windows executable execution, CorelDRAW 2019 import/cut behavior,
real bell geometry, viewing distance, vinyl/transfer tape, connected support versus separate letters,
and an approved physical application trial. Preview/SVG tests cannot approve these.
