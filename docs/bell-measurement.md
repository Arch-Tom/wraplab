# Bell measurements and approved material trial

The actual job is cut vinyl with a level, undistorted straight-on target. The implemented view assumes
an upright bell and distant horizontal orthographic observation. The measured-profile conformal
pattern and its strain diagnostics are experimental; research is not physical approval.

1. Establish a labelled TOP plane and chosen front meridian. Record axial heights down from that
   plane; do not confuse axial height with meridian distance. Measure only the usable region including
   the artwork band and margins. A labelled top at the start of this region is acceptable.
2. Start with roughly 6–10 diameter or circumference stations, densifying near profile bends.
   Enter the measurement type directly; preserve raw measurements, units, tools and uncertainty.
   Compare diameters in multiple directions for out-of-roundness. Decorative ribs/discontinuities
   and non-axisymmetric shapes are outside the smooth profile model.
3. Check withheld station measurements against PCHIP interpolation. It follows measurement noise
   rather than removing it. Save geometry as Bell — Current Project; shipped examples are illustrative.
   Presets hold geometry/measurement notes only; save a separate project to retain artwork/placement.
4. Record observer/camera height, distance, bell orientation and target region. Tilt/elevated view or
   close perspective changes the correspondence; this MVP does not silently compensate it.
5. Identify actual film, tape, connected mask versus separate letters, finish and installation method.
   The full placed page rectangle currently represents the mechanically coupled support. Required
   strain is not an established safe material range. Do not infer heat/stretch instructions from it.
6. Verify digital/Corel dimensions first. Prepare compensated and uncompensated lettering plus
   baseline/circle/grid registration specimens. Guides are ordinary paths and may cut; remove them
   from production cutter selection unless deliberately part of the trial.
7. Use a finish-compatible approved material on a surrogate or approved inconspicuous test area.
   Removable does not mean finish-safe. A paper trial can reveal fit issues but does not validate vinyl,
   adhesive or transfer tape. No irreversible engraving trial is part of this workflow.
8. Apply controls using the same reference marks and recorded viewing setup. Photograph straight on;
   compare baseline/shape distortion and inspect wrinkles, lift and compression/extension behavior.
   Record dimensional/visual errors and actual acceptance limits. The ideal preview alone proves none
   of these mechanical outcomes.
9. If unacceptable, revise region/support or manually plan permitted pieces/cuts and repeat the trial.
   Automatic segmentation, material optimization and redesign of customer lettering are not implemented.
