# Candidate-009 bench rehearsal

**EXPERIMENTAL — NOT PRODUCTION APPROVED**

Use unchanged private candidate-009. This checklist supplements its revised guides; it does not
change artwork, references, measurements or calibration marks. Begin on a safe fixture without
customer-bell contact. Choose acceptable position, appearance and effort criteria before testing.

## Prepare and print

- [ ] Open candidate-009 `START-HERE-REVISED.pdf`, `REGISTRATION-REPEATABILITY-CHECK.pdf`
  and `PHYSICAL-TRIAL-WORKSHEET-REVISED.pdf`. Use `GROUP-REFERENCE-TABLES-UNCHANGED.pdf`
  for the target coordinates.
- [ ] From `UNCHANGED_REFERENCES_DO_NOT_CUT/`, use the matching
  `REFERENCE_ONLY_B_<group>.pdf` and `REFERENCE_ONLY_C_<group>.pdf`, where `<group>` is
  `cantorland`, `lower_hebrew`, `upper_hebrew` or `star`. Start with CantorLand English.
  Match each to paper artwork from the unchanged Phase 2 art-only B/C candidate of that group.
  **Never send reference sheets or overlays to a cutter.**
- [ ] Print at **100% / actual size**, with fit-to-page disabled. Physically measure **both
  100 mm calibration axes**. Align and remeasure tiled pages. Stop if scale is wrong.
  Never resize compensation output to a four-inch flat height.
- [ ] Match at least three distinctive contour/counter features between paper artwork and its
  reference overlay. Preserve their relationship; label condition, group, orientation and source.
  Identify **L, R and TOP** from that matching reference. B retains full-layout page coordinates;
  C uses trimmed group pages. Do not exchange origins or use B's flat gaps to place C.

## Check the apparatus first

- [ ] Establish a persistent, independently checked witness datum/front/grid on a safe fixture,
  separate from the apparatus being reset. The bench is not automatically the original bell zero.
  Check straight height, level and front direction. Use **FRONT X** for a frame or **ARC** around
  the correct level ring for a permitted contact band; do not interchange them.
- [ ] Set L/R/TOP against the witness, record them, then dismantle/reset and rebuild the apparatus
  **at least three independent times**. Three readings of one setup do not count. Cover the group
  heights and horizontal travel. Check the actual pointer tip, direction, flex and stand-off, or
  the same seated band edge. Any contact method needs separate finish-compatibility permission.
- [ ] Record position variation, independent accuracy checks, stand-off, setup time and difficulty.
  Stop if witnesses move, zero cannot be recovered, depth/clearance cannot be checked, or uncertainty
  is too large to distinguish the comparison. Repeated resets alone do not prove accuracy.

## Rehearse and compare B/C

- [ ] **Optional planar connector demonstration:** independently set two points **108.076019 mm**
  apart. Check B English's intact printed L–R connector: **106.545138 mm**. Hold L; keep the
  connector straight and parallel without pulling. Expected shortfall is **1.530881 mm**, subject
  to measured uncertainty. This checks scale and a length constraint, not curved-bell fit.
- [ ] A flat board can check projected X/height. It cannot validate bell curvature or TOP depth.
  Only proceed to a curved-surface paper trial on a safe fixture whose relevant geometry has been
  independently shown to represent the nominal surface.
- [ ] Keep **L primary**, orient L→R toward the R target, and record R/TOP misses **without forcing
  the marks together**. Record pose, bending, stand-off, any restarts/reliefs and measurement method.
  An off-surface mark has no automatic arc reading; one front photo does not give a 3D residual.
- [ ] Use identical targets for B/C and alternate trial order. Record setup and placement effort,
  signed residuals, counter/letter integrity and appearance separately. Do not move targets or
  distort the paper to hide a miss. Stop if the pose or residual cannot be measured reproducibly.

## Before any customer-bell or material trial

- [ ] Confirm the original datum, current bell measurements/circularity, measured-domain coverage,
  stable support and independently checked reference method. Consequential changes require reviewed
  candidates; do not shift targets, rescale frozen patterns or extrapolate.
- [ ] Verify the actual film, transfer tape, finish compatibility and manufacturer guidance before
  material contact. Never cut against the bell. **Paper success does not establish vinyl fit,
  adhesive compatibility, transfer release, retention or durability.**
